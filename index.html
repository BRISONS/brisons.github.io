The BRISONS  

The BRISONS, or BRitish ISOgeNy dayS, is a recurring meetup of cryptographers working on isogeny-based cryptography in and around The United Kingdom.

Contact

To register for an event or to get regular updates, please contact research@borisfouotsa.com, l.maino@bham.ac.uk or chloe.martindale@bristol.ac.uk.
